Steepness (Steepness: Auto, Gentle, Medium, Steep, Extreme)
Skin editor (Default, Boy, Girl, Businessman, Angel, Demon, Chef, Psychedelic)
Level Editor (Essentially a terrain editor for your current mountain) 
Bots that ski with you (around 30)
Mountain editor (For current mountain - Height Drop, Ruggedness, Tree Density, Base Altitude)
Reshape (New seed for your mountain to be generated on)
Light and Weather (Switch between Day, Dusk and Night - Snowfall On/Off)

Snow Road (Special Mode)
- Road Width, Curviness, Holes, Trees on the road, Steepness
- Ski This Road (Ski the road you made based off of current settings)
- New Layout (Generates a new road based off of current settings)

Music On/Off button

Bottom Left, Player Information
- Speed (km/h)
- Altitude (m)
- Vertical Drop (m)

Bottom Left, Player Information - EXTRAS DURING SNOWROAD
- Progress (% to bottom)
- Time (time spent on current run)

Minimap, Upper right.

Controls, Bottom right
- A,D or arrow keys to carve/turn
- S to brake
- Space to jump
- R to respawn
- Scroll Wheel to zoom camera
- Scroll Wheel Pressed Down to rotate camera
