Free Roam Skiing Game! (made with claude, sorry for the anti-AI folks)
You can pick any mountain you'd like, ranging from the flattest to the steepest out there in real life! Or.. you could just go to Mount Everest and change the steepness to EXTREME!
A/D to turn, S to brake, Space to jump, R to respawn, Mouse scroll to zoom, Mouse wheel HELD to look around.
If you want to choose mountains, make your own, change your skin, blah blah etcetera etcetera Press menu on the upper left.
If you get tired of the music, Press the .Music On ♩. to change it to .Music Off ♩. Click it anytime to switch between on and off!
Those other skiers? Not real, those are BOTS! But don't worry, Ill soon find a way to get TRUE multiplayer in!
